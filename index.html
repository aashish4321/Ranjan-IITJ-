Teaching Philosophy

Understanding before memorising.

I believe education should prepare students for real life, not rote memorisation. Students should be able to apply what they learn in everyday situations, and understand why a concept works instead of just recalling it for a test. Hands-on learning, where observation, practice and theory work together, is the best way to learn, as my own DIY physics project showed me. I believe a teacher should be a facilitator who guides students rather than only lectures, letting them experiment first and come to the teacher when they are stuck. Curriculum should be flexible under NEP, so students can follow their interests and strengths. Assessment should be continuous, so one bad day never defines a student. Above all, I believe in a classroom built on mutual respect, where quizzes and small challenges keep students engaged.
